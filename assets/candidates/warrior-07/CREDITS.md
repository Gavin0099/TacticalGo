head, held hand/equipment and fork shield emblem are rigid original-pixel layers.

from the existing local HeroBody candidate; backing prompt/hash/source receipts
are retained under source/. No new generation or rewriting of that checkout.

voice-design auditions. No reference actor, character imitation or external
speech recording was used. Prompts, seeds, raw and trimmed/gain-normalized WAV,
pinned model/runtime hashes are in voice/manifest.json. Local existing
Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign model revision5c390979e4b93af5f2932f90742ca99c7dd04687,
original/converted Apache-2.0 declarations and retained licenses/model cards
under voice/source/. No paid API or live mobile model inference.

not validate acting, accent or perceived personality.
